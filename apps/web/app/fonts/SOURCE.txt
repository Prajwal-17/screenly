GeistVF.woff and GeistMonoVF.woff are restored unchanged from this repository's
master commit 64d570884aa3c81574ff3e120c31b18b482212e5. Both are committed and
served through next/font/local; development, type checks, and builds do not
fetch fonts from a CDN. Geist Sans is the application font; Geist Mono is
reserved for code. The loader variables are --font-geist-sans and
--font-geist-mono, distinct from Tailwind's --font-sans and --font-mono.
