Active shared family — REQ-038
-----------------------------
The screenshot-pinned UI uses the system-first stack backed by Geist Sans:
  -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, var(--font-geist-sans), "Segoe UI", sans-serif
GeistVF.woff (100–900) and GeistMonoVF.woff (100–900) are locally loaded in app/layout.tsx.
Geist Mono serves code. Both retain the original SIL OFL 1.1 license in OFL.txt.
Source: https://github.com/vercel/geist-font; restored unchanged from master commit
64d570884aa3c81574ff3e120c31b18b482212e5. Copyright 2024 The Geist Project Authors.
